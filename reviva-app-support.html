Frequently Asked Questions
What does Reviora do?

Reviora restores old or damaged photos, increases their resolution to HD, colorizes black-and-white photos, and performs full restoration. Simply choose your photo, select a restoration tool, and view the result within seconds.

Are my photos safe? Are they used to train AI models?

Your photos are uploaded to secure cloud storage and processed only to perform the action you request. We do not use your photos for AI model training, advertising, or profiling. For more information, please read our Privacy Policy.

Why are my results watermarked or low resolution?

You can start the restoration process and preview the result for free. A Premium subscription is required to download the full-resolution HD result without a watermark.

How can I manage or cancel my subscription?

Your subscription is billed through your App Store or Google Play account. You can manage or cancel it from your store account settings on your device. Your subscription will renew automatically unless auto-renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period.

The process failed. What should I do?

No payment or credit is deducted when a process fails, so you can try again. If the issue continues, send us an email with your device model, app version, and a short description of the problem.

How can I delete my data or account?

You can delete your results from within the app. You may also email us to request the permanent deletion of your account and all uploaded photos.
