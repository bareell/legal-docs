Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy explains how MyLuna ("the app", "we"), provided by Barbaros İhtiyar (the "Developer", data controller), collects, uses and protects information when you use the app. MyLuna helps you track your menstrual cycle, symptoms, pregnancy, pill intake and missed fasting days (Ramadan/kaza), and offers an AI assistant for general questions.

Short version: your health records are used only to provide the app to you. We do not sell or share your health data, we do not use it for advertising, we do not track you across other apps (no App Tracking Transparency prompt) and the app contains no advertising. You can export or delete all your data at any time in the app.

1. Information we collect

Account and identifiers

  • Anonymous device account: you can use MyLuna without signing up. The app creates an anonymous account bound to a random identifier generated on your device.
  • Optional account: if you create an account we store your email address and a securely hashed password, so your records can be synced and restored on a new phone.

Health data you enter (special category data)

  • Period start and end dates, flow and cycle settings (cycle and period length),
  • Symptoms, moods and other daily logs, and personal notes,
  • Pregnancy tracking details (for example due date or pregnancy week),
  • Birth control pill schedule and intake logs,
  • Ramadan fasting and missed fasting (kaza) records.

This data is stored on our own servers (api.myluna.app) so it can be synced and backed up. Predictions such as your next period or fertile window are calculated from it.

AI assistant questions

The questions you type into the "Ask" assistant and the answers given.

Notifications

If you allow notifications we store a Firebase Cloud Messaging device token and your reminder preferences.

Purchases

If MyLuna Plus is offered, it is paid through the Apple App Store or Google Play and managed by RevenueCat. We receive only your subscription status (plan, active or expired, expiry date), never your payment card details.

Diagnostics

Crash reports and technical diagnostics (device model, OS version, app version, error traces) are collected through Sentry. They are configured not to include your health records, email address or other personal content.

2. Purposes and legal basis

  • Providing the app (storing and syncing your records, calculating predictions, reminders): performance of our agreement with you; for health data, your explicit consent given in the app (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a), KVKK Art. 6).
  • AI assistant answers: your explicit consent, given when you use the assistant.
  • Account security, abuse prevention, crash diagnostics: our legitimate interest in a safe and stable app.
  • Subscriptions: performance of the purchase agreement.

You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your data or account; this does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling and we do not sell or rent it.

3. AI assistant

Answers in the "Ask" assistant are generated with Anthropic Claude. When you ask a question, the text of your question (and the earlier messages of that conversation) is sent to Anthropic to produce the answer. Anthropic processes this data on our behalf and, under its commercial terms, does not use it to train its models. Please avoid writing information that directly identifies you. Answers are general information, not medical advice.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Your records are kept while your account exists.
  • When you delete your account in the app (Settings → My account → Delete account), all your data (records, notes, AI conversations, device tokens and account details) is permanently deleted from our systems. Residual copies in encrypted backups are removed within a reasonable time, as part of the regular backup cycle.
  • Crash reports are kept by Sentry only for a limited period and are deleted within a reasonable time.

6. Your rights

Under KVKK (Art. 11) and GDPR you have the right to know whether your data is processed, to access, correct, delete and export it, to object to processing, to withdraw consent and to complain to a supervisory authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK). You can:

  • Export your records using the export option in Settings,
  • Delete your account and all data from Settings → My account → Delete account,
  • Turn notifications off in the app or in device settings,
  • Contact us at barbaros.ihtiyar.developerr@gmail.com for any other request. We respond within 30 days.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as secure hashes and access to servers is restricted. No method is 100% secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information.

8. Children

MyLuna is not intended for children under 13, and users under 16 (or the age of digital consent in their country) should use it only with the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Not a medical device

MyLuna is not a medical device and must not be used as contraception or for diagnosis. See our Terms of Use.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version; significant changes will be announced in the app.

"Sor" asistanındaki yanıtlar Anthropic Claude ile üretilir. Soru sorduğunuzda sorunuzun metni (ve o konuşmanın önceki mesajları) yanıt üretilmesi için Anthropic'e gönderilir. Anthropic bu verileri bizim adımıza işler ve ticari koşulları gereği modellerini eğitmek için kullanmaz. Lütfen sizi doğrudan tanımlayan bilgiler yazmaktan kaçının.
