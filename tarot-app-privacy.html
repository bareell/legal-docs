Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how Arcana ("the app", "we"), provided by Barbaros İhtiyar (the "Developer"), collects, uses and protects information when you use the app. Arcana offers tarot readings, a daily card, moon calendar and personal insights interpreted with the help of AI.

Short version: we collect only what is needed to give you readings and keep your history. We do not sell your data, we do not track you across other apps or websites and the app contains no advertising.
Data we collectHow we use itAI processingService providersRetentionYour rightsContact

1. Information we collect

Account and identifiers

  • Guest identifier: you can use the app without signing up. We create an anonymous guest account bound to a random device identifier generated by the app.
  • Optional account: if you create an account we store your email address and a securely hashed password, or the identifier provided by Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In.
  • Optional profile details: your name, birth date and focus area if you choose to enter them, used to personalise readings. Your zodiac sign is derived from your birth date and is not stored separately.

Content you create

  • The questions you ask, the cards drawn and the resulting interpretations (your reading history),
  • Follow-up questions you write about a reading and the answers to them,
  • Personal notes you add to readings,
  • For compatibility readings, the partner name and birth date you enter.

Notifications

If you allow notifications we store a Firebase Cloud Messaging device token and your notification preferences so we can send your daily card and reminders.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are paid through the Apple App Store or Google Play and managed by RevenueCat. We receive only your subscription status (plan, active or expired, expiry date). We never receive your payment card details.

Diagnostics

Crash reports and technical diagnostics (device model, OS version, app version, error traces) are collected through Firebase Crashlytics to keep the app stable.

2. How we use information

  • To generate your readings and answer your follow-up questions,
  • To keep your reading history, notes and insights available across sessions,
  • To send the notifications you enabled,
  • To verify your email and secure your account,
  • To unlock Premium features and enforce fair daily usage limits,
  • To diagnose crashes and prevent abuse.

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling and we do not sell or rent it.

3. AI processing

Interpretations are generated with Google Gemini. To create a reading we send the drawn cards, your question, the language of the app and, where relevant, profile details you chose to provide and the previous messages of that reading's conversation. This data is used only to produce the answer you requested. Readings are generated for entertainment and self-reflection; see our Terms of Use.

5. Data retention

  • Account data and reading history are kept while your account is active.
  • Guest accounts that are not used for 90 days are deleted automatically together with their readings.
  • In-app notifications are removed after 90 days.
  • When you delete your account in the app, your profile, readings, notes and device tokens are deleted from our systems.

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or KVKK) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data and to object to processing. You can:

  • Edit or clear profile details in Settings,
  • Delete your account from Settings → Delete account,
  • Turn notifications off in the app or in your device settings,
  • Contact us at barbaros.ihtiyar.developerr@gmail.com for any other request.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as secure hashes. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. Children

Arcana is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version; significant changes will be announced in the app.

Hesap ve kimlikler

Oluşturduğunuz içerik

  • Sorduğunuz sorular, çekilen kartlar ve yorumlar (okuma geçmişiniz),

Bildirimler

Bildirimlere izin verirseniz günün kartını ve hatırlatmaları gönderebilmek için Firebase Cloud Messaging cihaz anahtarınızı ve bildirim tercihlerinizi saklarız.

Tanılama

Uygulamayı kararlı tutmak için çökme raporları ve teknik tanılama verileri (cihaz modeli, işletim sistemi ve uygulama sürümü, hata izleri) Firebase Crashlytics ile toplanır.
